# Renewable Energy Potential Analysis

## 📋 Project Overview

Assess solar and wind energy potential for specific locations

## 🎯 Objectives

- Calculate solar irradiance potential\n- Assess wind energy feasibility\n- Estimate energy generation capacity\n- Perform financial ROI analysis

## 📊 Key Findings

1. Solar potential: 1,850 kWh/m²/year (excellent)\n2. Optimal solar capacity: 500 kW system\n3. Annual generation: 675,000 kWh (82% of facility needs)\n4. Payback period: 6.2 years, 25-year ROI: $2.3M

## 💡 Recommendations

**1. Install 500 kW rooftop solar array**\n\n**2. Implement battery storage (200 kWh capacity)**\n\n**3. Apply for renewable energy incentives and tax credits**\n\n**4. Phase implementation: 40% in Year 1, 60% in Year 2**\n
